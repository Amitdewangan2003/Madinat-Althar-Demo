About Us

A trading house built for the Gulf's supply chain

Madinat Althar General Trading L.L.C. is headquartered in Dubai and works at the point where global supply meets regional demand. We identify quality producers abroad, bring their goods into the UAE through our own warehousing operation, and distribute them onward across the GCC.

Our focus stays narrow by design — agricultural commodities, processed food, and engineering products — so every shipment benefits from category-specific sourcing relationships and storage practices, rather than generic logistics.
